Hackers Steal $351.6 Million from Bitget Crypto Exchange

BleepingComputer • The Hacker News • 25 Sep 2026

WHAT HAPPENED: Suspected North Korean threat actors successfully breached Bitget's hot and warm wallets, siphoning out $351.6 million. The attack exploited backend wallet systems and manipulated transactions to trigger withdrawal authorizations.

WHY IT MATTERS: This breach underscores the vulnerability of large-scale crypto platforms to persistent state-sponsored attackers, especially those from sanctioned regimes.
